About This Project
This environmental issues reporter helps communities identify and address environmental problems. By reporting issues, we can work together to create a cleaner, healthier planet.
How To Report
Fill out the form with details about the environmental issue you've observed. Be specific about location, type of issue, and provide a clear description. Your email is required for verification purposes only.
Privacy Policy
Your personal information is kept confidential. Email addresses are used only for verification and follow-up on reported issues. We never share your data with third parties.
